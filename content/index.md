# Hello ☕️

I’m a Senior Software Engineer at [Increff](https://www.increff.com/), where I build backend systems for the Transport Management System (TMS)—a 0→1 product focused on multi-carrier logistics and supply chain optimization.

Welcome to my digital garden, where curiosity grows in every direction. Some days I’m exploring **distributed systems** and **engineering leadership**; other days I’m captivated by **psychology**, the vastness of **interstellar space**, football tactics, or searching for the next pair to add to my sneaker collection. Every note here is a snapshot of something that made me stop, think, and learn.

I believe learning should be visible and messy — so feel free to wander through my half-finished thoughts, follow the connections, and maybe leave me a note if something resonates.
