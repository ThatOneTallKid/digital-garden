Anything you read on this website is ideated by a human: me.
Nowadays you can write anything about anything without even getting an inch deep into that topic. All thanks to LLMs. And thus,  I don’t find much satisfaction in publishing words that aren’t backed by genuine experience or curiosity.
I am constantly trying to improve my ability to articulate my ideas in my own words.
That said, I’m not against AI. In fact, I use it almost every day. I see it as an editor, a research assistant, and occasionally a pair programmer—not as a ghostwriter.

### **What I use AI for**

- Proofreading, grammar, and improving clarity.
- Research-heavy exploration and understanding unfamiliar topics.
- Maintaining the codebase that powers this website.
- Brainstorming alternative perspectives or identifying gaps in my thinking.

### **What I don’t use AI for**

- Writing the first draft of an article.
- Generating original opinions, experiences, or stories.
- Publishing AI-generated paragraphs as my own writing.
